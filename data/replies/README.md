# Vendor right of reply

Before each cycle is published, every assessed vendor is sent its complete
scorecard — every check value and evidence note — with a reply window.

- Vendor rebuttals are published **verbatim** in this directory as
  `<cycle>/<slug>.md`, alongside the scores they dispute.
- Vendors do **not** get a veto over their entry or their scores.
  Right of reply is a correction mechanism, not pre-approval.
- If a rebuttal demonstrates that a shipped capability was misjudged, the
  correction is applied in the next cycle and recorded in
  [CHANGES.md](../../CHANGES.md); the rebuttal stays published either way.

No vendor replies have been received yet. The August 2026 cycle predates
this process; scorecards for the September 2026 cycle will be sent to all
ten vendors before publication. Vendors can reply via
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/SourcingBench/SourcingTools/issues) or
the [SourcingTools.org contact page](https://sourcingtools.org/contact/).
